## Progression and failure classification

```gherkin
Scenario: A resource-blocked shape does not stop independent shapes
  Given the10k giant-group case crossed the frozen resource limit
  And its owned native group stopped and raw failure evidence was retained
  When other10k shapes have all their1k seed/role/holdout cases passed
  Then those shapes remain admissible under the same native guards
  And the giant case is resource-censored, not passed or replayed
  And remaining giant cases at10k/50k/100k are explicitly not admitted

Scenario: Advance a workload, not a whole size
  Given every required baseline/calibration seed and semantic holdout for one shape passed at smaller sizes
  When that shape advances to50k or100k
  Then its exact same-shape predecessor ledger supplies admission proof
  But evidence from another shape or missing holdouts cannot qualify it

Scenario: Operational failure is not resource censoring
  Given a focus, semantic, provenance, startup, owner-admission or unresolved-cleanup failure
  When the controller evaluates the result
  Then it stops for reconciliation without another dispatch
  And it cannot disguise the failure as a resource-censored branch
```

A verified resource censor requires actual child exit1/no signal, failed case status,
explicit native resource-watchdog cause, a finite sampled threshold crossing, and stopped
owned cleanup with no remaining tracked identities. Only the known resource-stop outcome
forms are admitted. Unknown/ambiguous errors stop globally rather than broadening this rule.
Branch censoring stops remaining cases at the same or larger sizes; it does not assert they
were tested or failed. A lower-size shape must have all eight role/seed/holdout process cases
passed before advancing. No automatic retries or operational-failure resume are implemented.
